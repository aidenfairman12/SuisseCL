## Assumptions (docs/decisions/assumptions_log.md)
| A-040 | 2026-11-19 | Cooled buildings have a reversible heat pump: a cooling device with the same nominal electric power, efficiency and supply temperature (8 C) as the heating device, modeled in CityLearn as a second device object of equal rating; the combined action (negative = cooling) makes heating and cooling exclusive in a step. | judgement; no cooling-system data for these buildings | accepted |
| A-041 | 2026-11-19 | Cooling COP from CityLearn's Carnot-fraction form (heating-device efficiency 0.36 to 0.46 depending on the building); cooling is switched off (capacity zero) when the outdoor temperature is below 15 C because the formula returns COP 20 below the supply temperature. | CityLearn `HeatPump.get_cop`; guard is an agent choice | accepted |
| A-042 | 2026-11-19 | Comfort of cooled buildings: lower limit 21 C; upper limit 24 C outside June to September (local months) and 26 C in June to September; the upper limit is never below the thermostat path (baseline = heat to 21 C, cool to 26 C). Hard constraints in the exact program (the MPC uses soft ones). | judgement (CityLearn default cooling set point 26 C, D-018 heating set point 21 C) | accepted; owner has not confirmed the 26 C set point |
| A-043 | 2026-11-19 | Uncooled buildings have no cooling and float in summer; their overheating is reported as degree-hours above 26 C (threshold also reported for cooled buildings); they keep the Phase B comfort band (21 to 24 C, never below the thermostat path). | judgement | accepted |
| A-044 | 2026-11-19 | Adoption level by whole building, shares of the 46 dwelling units: low = MFH-small (10.9 %), medium = MFH-medium (30.4 %), high = MFH-large (52.2 %); sensitivity high_alt = MFH-medium + MFH-small + the three houses (47.8 %). A level confounds adoption with building identity. | proposal of docs/phases/phase_c_proposal_updated.md; owner has not confirmed | accepted as default |
| A-045 | 2026-11-19 | RC parameters (H, g, C, internal gain) and the building areas (calibrated to 2023 heat) do not change with the climate scenario: same buildings, no shading, no night ventilation, no latent load, no humidity effect on comfort. | judgement | accepted |
| A-046 | 2026-11-19 | Household base load (non-shiftable load and DHW electricity) is the same in every climate scenario except that the DHW COP follows the outdoor temperature; no climate-driven change in plug loads or hot water use. | judgement | accepted |
| A-047 | 2026-11-19 | PV capacity and battery size per building are the Phase B values (ratio 1.0, 1 kWh per kWp) in every cell; only the PV production changes with the scenario weather. | Phase B A-011 to A-017 | accepted |

## Issues (docs/decisions/issues_and_risks.md)
| ISS-043 | 2026-11-19 | Cooling demand, cooling COP and cooling adoption are modeled or assumed; no measured cooling exists for these buildings and the model has no latent load, shading or ventilation, so cooling energy is probably overstated for night-ventilated houses and understated for humid heat waves. | C (WP2) | Open limitation | No |
| ISS-044 | 2026-11-19 | The climate datasets use the 2023 areas and rounded dataset areas (rc_params_2023.json) while oracle_rc recalibrates the area from the derived annual heat; the heating-only climate program therefore differs from oracle_rc by under 0.01 % of the bill on the same inputs (area rounding at the first decimal). | C (WP2) | Closed (documented) | No |
| ISS-045 | 2026-11-19 | CityLearn applies the 20 cap in the cooling COP below the supply temperature (COP 20 for outdoor temperature below 8 C); cooling is therefore disabled below 15 C outdoor (A-041). A simulation where cooling is useful below 15 C outdoor is not representable. | C (WP2) | Open limitation | No |
| ISS-046 | 2026-11-19 | The replay is exact (0.000 % of the saving) for the cooled cell up to step 5,100 of 2023 and for the full year as far as run (see EXP entry of the parent); the oracle and the CityLearn building share one RC implementation (`step_temperature`), so the replay checks the action mapping, not the physics. | C (WP2) | Open limitation | No |
