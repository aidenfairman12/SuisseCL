## Model (docs/models/model_registry.md)
- **M-007** Two-zone room-unit extension of M-006 (single-node RC with cooling): a building with cooled fraction f is two independent RC nodes with the same per-m2 parameters, a cooled zone (area f A) and an uncooled zone ((1 - f) A); one heating action whose heat is split by area; cooling removes heat from the cooled zone only; cooling capacity proportional to f; the area-weighted mean temperature follows the single-node equation with net heat Q - Qc (exact, tested). Used by `oracle_climate`, `climate_controllers`, CityLearn subclass `RCDynamicsBuilding`. Limits: no heat exchange between zones, no schedule, no latent load.

## Assumptions (assumptions_log.md)
- **A-058**: Archetype B cooled fraction f = 0.4 of the floor area of a cooled building (default), sensitivity 0.2 and 0.6 on `gwl3.0_hw99_high` only; no source (literature found by search, not read: fewer than one in ten Swiss homes has fixed air conditioning and portable units are popular; no Swiss source found for room-level use). Adoption levels and the buildings that are cooled are the same as archetype A (whole buildings by dwelling-unit share, A-0xx of WP2).
- **A-059**: The two zones do not exchange heat and have the same per-m2 parameters as the building (H, g, C, internal gain); both start at the 21 C set point. Heating is one action whose heat is split in proportion to area; the heating thermostat acts on the area-weighted mean temperature; the room unit cools its own zone to 26 C when its free temperature would exceed it, never in the same step as heating.
- **A-060**: Room-unit capacity is proportional to f (cooling device nominal power = f x the heating device's); efficiency, 8 C supply temperature, Carnot-fraction COP and the 15 C outdoor cut-off as archetype A; no operating schedule (the unit runs whenever the cooled zone would exceed 26 C), no dehumidification.
- **A-061**: Comfort and overheating accounting for archetype B: cooling season upper limit 26 C in the cooled zone (soft, 2 CHF per K h in the MPC), 24 C in the uncooled zone as in uncooled buildings (soft, 0.05 CHF per K h, the WP3 rerun fix); degree-hours above 26 C of a building = f x cooled zone + (1 - f) x uncooled zone (area-weighted), zone values reported raw; comfort cold hours on the mean temperature and per zone.

## Issues (issues_and_risks.md)
- **ISS-049**: Archetype B is an idealisation: independent zones without heat exchange, units that run whenever needed, no schedule and no dehumidification; real room units serve selected rooms at selected hours. Cooling energy per cooled building differs from archetype A by construction (ratio 0.40 for the thermostat, 0.39 to 0.42 for the optimum, equal to f). Open limitation.
- **ISS-050**: The uncooled zones of cooled buildings (and uncooled buildings) overheat strongly in the unshaded model (district degree-hours above 26 C with the thermostat 46,168 in 2023 weather for archetype B against 41,220 for A with the high level; the uncooled zone of the cooled building alone 8,246 K h in 2023 weather); an indicator, not a prediction (same cause as ISS-046). Open limitation.
- **ISS-051**: The replay of the two-zone optimum in CityLearn checks the action mapping, not the physics, because the LP and CityLearn use the same RC step (as ISS for WP2); the full-year replay of archetype B was not run (5,100 steps including the heat wave: equal to the cent).
