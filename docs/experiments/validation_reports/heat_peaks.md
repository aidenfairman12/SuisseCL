## Heat-pump capacity cap (swiss/experiments/heat_cap_check.py)

The thermostat asks for the heat that holds the set point; the space-heating heat pump delivers at most its rated power (1.1 x the highest hourly space-heating electricity of the derived 2023 dataset). Where a building sits at its cap, its peak equals the rating in every climate, so its peak change is 0.0 % (an uncapped heat pump would show a larger peak and a larger fall); the district peak may be slightly understated in the cap hours. The cap hours fall with warming; the room temperature never goes below the comfort lower bound. Sizing is unchanged by this analysis (assumption on device sizes: see the assumptions log).
