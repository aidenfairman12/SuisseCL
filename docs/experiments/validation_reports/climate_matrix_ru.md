# Phase C scenario matrix, archetype B (room units): climate x cooling adoption (WP2b)

ARCHETYPE B: in a cooled building only a fraction of the floor area (default 0.4, assumption A-058, no source) is cooled by room units (two independent zones, capacity proportional); the rest of the building floats. Cell names end in `_ru` (`_ru0.2`, `_ru0.6` sensitivities). The difference-method rows compare with the heating-only cell of archetype A. Generated by `swiss/experiments/climate_matrix.py aggregate --arch ru` from `swiss/experiments/climate_results_ru/`. Nothing here is typed by hand. Every cell is an S3 district (assumed PV, battery, RC building; export 8 Rp/kWh) with cooling in the cooled buildings; climate scenarios are CH2025 delta-change years built on observed 2023 weather (`climate_scenarios.md`); `*_hw99` = the 99th-percentile heat-wave week inserted in week 29. Cooling demand, COP, device and adoption are modelled or assumed (see the registry drafts), none is measured. Reference = thermostat (heat to 21 C, cool to 26 C where a cooling device exists). Year ceilings are perfect-foresight exact optima (comfort band 21 to 24 C, 26 C in the cooling season for cooled buildings); the held-out-week ceilings evaluate the same optimum on the eight test weeks.

## Limits
- Cooling is modelled (A-040 to A-047): reversible heat pump, cooling off below 15 C outdoors, 26 C cooling season set point; adoption levels are labelled defaults (whole buildings by dwelling-unit share); one station (Kloten) and 2023 weather; heat waves come from model chains made hourly (`climate_scenarios.md`).
- The household load is a synthetic composite (ISS-040); the MPC uses known weather (A-028) and forecasts of load, DHW demand and PV trained outside the test weeks.
- Exact optima are perfect-foresight upper bounds; PV allocation shares are a bookkeeping rule (`climate_metrics.py`), not a physical attribution.
