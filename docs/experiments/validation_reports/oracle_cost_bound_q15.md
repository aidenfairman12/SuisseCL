## How to read it
- A finer step lets the optimum shift energy at a finer grain, but with a price that changes only on the hour and hourly weather the extra headroom is small; the table shows how small.
- The 15-minute thermostat and zero-action baselines differ slightly from the hourly ones because the 15-minute series redistribute the same hourly energy.
