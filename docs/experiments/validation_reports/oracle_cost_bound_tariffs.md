**These are labelled scenarios ("what if prices varied more"), not statements about current Swiss prices.** The realistic baseline is S0 (EKZ standard two-tier tariff 2023). Time-variable products exist in Switzerland only as pilots or opt-in offers. The EKZ pilot tariffs (time-of-use grid fee, dynamic grid fee) started on 1 October 2023, so only the structure is carried over to the other months of 2023; the 2024 dynamic fee follows 2024 grid conditions, not the 2023 weather. The last row uses the Swiss day-ahead market price of 2023 (Energy-Charts, CC BY 4.0 from Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de) as a retail pass-through with an assumed exchange rate (0.97 CHF/EUR) and its own export price (the spot price); it is revenue neutral to S0 so only its shape differs. Other rows: energy component is the 2023 two-tier energy tariff; export price 8 Rp/kWh (assumed, A-015).

## How to read it
- S1 to S3 contain assumed PV, battery and thermal model (A-011 to A-020), so their numbers describe the assumed scenario.
- The bill is net of export revenue and differs between rungs and tariffs, so shares and francs are not comparable across rows without care.
- Perfect foresight: a ceiling, not a forecast. S0 under the standard tariff is the 0.84 % of Phase A.
