## What changes for controllers
- Phase A (M-001): the demand profile is the derived hourly series with its noise (defrost, hot-water leakage, SPF error); a controller can only act through tanks; no comfort quantity exists.
- RC (M-005): the demand responds to outdoor temperature and sun with a time constant of about 30 h, is smoother (see hour-to-hour change), and the building mass becomes a storage the controller can use within a comfort band; comfort violations exist. The exact ceiling for S3 shows the building mass adds 1.6 points on top of tanks and battery (D-039).
- Not captured by either: part-load and cycling effects (ISS-034), cooling and overheating behaviour (the RC model has no cooling: indoor temperature exceeds 24 C in about 2,700 hours a year under a heating-only thermostat), between-building differences in mass and insulation (pooled priors, A-018), real occupant behaviour.
