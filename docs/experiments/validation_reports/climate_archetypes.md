# Cooling archetypes: A central against B room units (Phase C, WP2b)

Generated by `swiss/experiments/climate_archetypes.py` from `swiss/experiments/climate_results/climate_summary.json` (archetype A) and `swiss/experiments/climate_results_ru/climate_summary_ru.json` (archetype B). Nothing here is typed by hand.

**Reading rules.** (1) Archetype A cools the whole cooled building to 26 C (one thermal node, central or ducted equivalent, WP2). Archetype B (room units, split or portable) cools only a fraction f of the floor area of the cooled building (default f = 0.4, assumption A-058 without a source; sensitivity 0.2 and 0.6); the rest floats; the two zones are independent thermal nodes with the same per-m2 parameters (no heat exchange between them, assumption), heating is one action split by area, and the cooling capacity is proportional to f. **Cooling energy per cooled building therefore differs between the archetypes by construction**, not as a finding. (2) Neither archetype is validated against Swiss field data of cooling energy; both are modelled. (3) The heating-only district (adoption none) is the same in both archetypes. (4) Degree-hours above 26 C: "building" = area-weighted sum of the zone values (f x cooled zone + (1 - f) x uncooled zone); the zone columns are raw K h summed over the room-unit buildings; a cooled zone is held at or below 26 C by the thermostat, so its value is the overshoot of the controller, not of the building; the overheating of uncooled floor area is a model artefact of the unshaded single-node room (ISS-046) and is an indicator, not a prediction. (5) Shares of the ceiling are on the eight held-out weeks (week 29 holds the heat wave in the `*_hw99` scenarios), MPC = weather-conditioned forecasts with weather taken as known (A-028).

## Limits
- Archetype B is a two-zone idealisation: no heat exchange between the zones, heating split by area, units sized in proportion to the cooled area, no schedule (units run whenever the cooled zone would exceed 26 C), no dehumidification. Real room units are used in selected rooms and hours; the model cools a share of the area all the time it needs it.
- Cooled fraction, set point (26 C), device efficiency and the 15 C cut-off are assumptions (A-040 to A-047, A-058 onward); no Swiss source was found for set points or room-level use.
- Everything else (household load synthetic ISS-040, unsourced tanks ISS-032, known weather A-028, one station, delta-change climate) is as in `climate_matrix.md`.
