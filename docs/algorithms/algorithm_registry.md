# Algorithm / controller registry

All Phase A controllers are stock CityLearn 3.3.0 classes (`cl/` venv, `pip` package; clone commit 0eb78bc). No learning algorithm is used in Phase A (plan 6.2 A8: no complex custom RL unless needed to show the environment works). ALG-005 to ALG-007 were added on 2026-11-18 (D-023, D-030) as a post-Phase-A extension: reinforcement learning on the Phase A environment. SAC/MARLISA code from the earlier US-cooling track (`my_data/`) is not reused here.

| ID | Name | Source / implementation | Objective | Observations used | Actions | Hyper-parameters | Training | Seeds | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ALG-001 | ZeroActionBaselineAgent | `citylearn.agents.baseline.ZeroActionBaselineAgent` | uncontrolled reference: storage never charged/discharged | none | 0 for every action | none | none | n/a (deterministic) | not a realistic operator; tanks idle |
| ALG-002 | BusinessAsUsualAgent | `citylearn.agents.baseline.BusinessAsUsualAgent` (version `immediate-full-charge-v1`) | stock BAU: EV immediate full charge, earliest appliance start, PV-oriented storage rule | storage SOC, net consumption | derived from its rules (no EV/appliance/battery here) | defaults | none | n/a | oriented to EV/battery/PV assets that this district does not have; expected (and checked) to behave like ALG-001 |
| ALG-003 | BasicRBC | `citylearn.agents.rbc.BasicRBC` | heat-pump-storage rule: charge 9.1 %/h of capacity between 22:00-08:00, discharge 8 %/h otherwise (documented in class docstring; thresholds are CityLearn's) | hour | heating_storage, dhw_storage per building | none | none | n/a | fixed clock rule, ignores tariff structure, weather and weekends |
| ALG-004 | HourRBC with tariff-aware action map | `citylearn.agents.rbc.HourRBC`, custom `action_map` from `swiss/experiments/run_baselines.py::tariff_map` | charge thermal storage at rate r in local hours with low EKZ tariff, discharge at rate r in high-tariff hours (CityLearn hours 9-20 = local 08-19), idle at hour 8 | hour | heating_storage, dhw_storage per building | r in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} of capacity per hour | none | n/a | HourRBC keys on hour only, so weekends are (incorrectly) treated like weekdays; rates r are assumptions, not optimised |
| ALG-005 | SAC, one central agent | `swiss.experiments.rl.SACBootstrap` (CityLearn 3.3.0 `SAC` subclass that bootstraps at window ends) | minimise reward R-001 or the cost-dominant variant for all six buildings with one agent | 25: hour, day type, outdoor temperature, electricity price and three price forecasts, net consumption and tank fill levels of all buildings | 12 slots: heating and hot-water tank charge or discharge of each building | library defaults; 2,000 random-action warm-up steps | random 4-week windows from the training weeks (D-030); 150,000 steps planned, stopped at 11 h wall-clock | 0 to 2 | one year of data; synthetic tanks (D-017a); the cost saving available to any controller is below 0.9 % of the bill (oracle) |
| ALG-006 | SAC, one agent per building | `swiss.experiments.rl.SACBootstrap` with `central_agent=False` | each agent minimises its share of the district reward | 10 per building: hour, day type, outdoor temperature, electricity price and three price forecasts, net consumption and tank fill levels of its own building | 2 slots per building | library defaults; 2,000 random-action warm-up steps | as ALG-005; 100,000 steps planned; about five times slower per step | 0 to 2 | as ALG-005; agents do not see other buildings |
| ALG-007 | MARLISA | `swiss.experiments.rl.MARLISABootstrap` (CityLearn 3.3.0 `MARLISA` subclass) | cooperative multi-agent version of SAC with information sharing between buildings | 10 per building plus the coordination variables | 2 slots per building | library defaults; 2,000 random-action warm-up steps | as ALG-006; evaluated at the end only (mid-training evaluation mutates its buffers) | 0 to 2 | as ALG-006 |
| ALG-008 | Exact perfect-foresight optimum (linear program) | `swiss/experiments/oracle.py` (Phase A, tanks only, EL-0049) and `swiss/experiments/oracle_pvbat.py` (S1 and S2: tanks, battery, PV net, export price) | upper bound on what any controller can save in the simulator; used by the ceiling-first gate (D-032) | one LP per building over the year with HiGHS (scipy 1.17.1); mirrors CityLearn 3.3.0 tank and battery rules; replayed in CityLearn | zero-action net consumption, prices, COP, tank and battery parameters | action schedule, predicted bill | not a controller: it knows the whole year | replay equals the program to 0.01 CHF or better (Phase A 299.08 CHF, S1 and S2 18,334.05 and 16,467.18 CHF at 8 Rp/kWh) | no peak or ramp terms, no forecast error, no degradation | optimisation |
| ALG-009 | Thermostat baseline for RC scenarios | `swiss.experiments.thermostat.ThermostatAgent` (uses the RC state of the building) | S3 counterpart of the zero-action baseline: heat pump heats to the 21 C setpoint every step, limited by its nominal output; tanks and battery idle | action = heat needed to reach the setpoint / nominal output x COP | indoor temperature state, weather | 1 heating action per building | none | matches the model baseline from step 2 on (swiss/tests/test_oracle_rc.py); the first two steps show a start-up transient of at most 0.4 kWh | zero heating action would let the house cool, so zero-action is not the S3 baseline; reads building internals, so it is a reference, not a deployable controller | rule |

Evaluation protocol (all): one deterministic episode, 2023 local year, 8,759 steps, central agent, schema `swiss/datasets/swiss_district_2023/schema.json`, results `swiss/experiments/results/baselines_2023.csv`.
