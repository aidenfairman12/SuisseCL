CityLearn metrics guide

How to read this. Every metric is a lower-is-better score. Most are divided by a reference called the conventional counterfactual (per the CityLearn docs: the same buildings without storage and without partial-load control, with PV kept), so 1.0 means "same as that reference", below 1 is better and above 1 is worse. The four groups below answer four different questions, and a controller can win one and lose another, so no single column is "the best" overall.
Shading is relative within each row: darker = better★ = best in row

Temperature rows are raw degrees °C, not ratios to BAU. Source: CityLearn env.evaluate(), district level.
